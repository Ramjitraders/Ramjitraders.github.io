Bitumen Washers

A bituminous sealing compound bonded to a galvanised steel or aluminium disc. On fastening, the compound compresses into every micro-gap — a permanent, maintenance-free watertight seal at every point.

  • Galvanised steel / aluminium base
  • Reflective Flake series — UV & weather resistant
  • Sizes M5–M20 · OD 15–50 mm · 1.5–5 mm
  • Metal roofing, cladding & solar mounting
  • Zero maintenance — seals for life

UV · Ozone · Chemical Resistant

EPDM Washers

High-performance synthetic rubber engineered for extreme service. It holds its seal thermal-cycle after thermal-cycle — where neoprene and natural rubber crack, EPDM endures. The global long-life standard.

  • Service range −40 °C to +120 °C
  • 25–30 year service life
  • Roofing, HVAC, automotive & marine
  • Sizes M5–M20 · custom compounding
