Bitumen Sealing Washers

A bituminous sealing compound bonded to a galvanised steel or aluminium disc. On fastening, the compound compresses into every micro-gap — a permanent, maintenance-free watertight seal at every screw point.

  • Galvanised steel / aluminium base
  • Reflective Flake series — UV & weather resistant
  • Sizes M5–M20 · OD 15–50 mm · 1.5–5 mm thickness
  • Metal roofing, trapezoidal profiles, cladding & solar mounting
  • Zero maintenance — seals for 25–30+ years

EPDM Composite Washers

High-performance synthetic rubber bonded to stainless or galvanised backing, engineered for extreme weather service. It holds elasticity thermal-cycle after thermal-cycle without cracking.

  • Service range −40 °C to +120 °C
  • 25–30 year service life — UV & ozone proof
  • Metal roofing, HVAC, facade engineering & automotive
  • Sizes M5–M20 · Custom vulcanisation & compounding
  • Compatible with self-drilling & wood construction screws
